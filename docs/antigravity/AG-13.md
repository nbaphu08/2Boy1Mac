# AG-13 — Cinematic transitions (no hitches, continuous camera, no Earth on the Moon)

**For:** Antigravity · **Issued by:** Opus · **Humans' feedback (15/11):** transitions still stutter — especially **space → Moon landing** and **site → site** — and feel "plastic"/uncinematic; and **remove Earth from the lunar sky** (humans' decision).
**Branch:** `ag/13-transitions` from latest `main`. **Hard stop: hand off by 15:30** (we must record the demo video and submit by 20:00). If something isn't done by then, hand off what is stable.
Rules as always: only `components/journey/three/**` + `public/media/three/**`; **do NOT generate posters**; perf tiers + reduced motion; typecheck, lint, build pass; short handoff, then STOP.

## Measured (Opus, Playwright + Chrome, 1280×720, continuous wheel scroll from `deep-space` onward)
Median frame 16.7 ms, p95 16.8 ms — fine — but **long frames**:
| scroll p | frame time | where |
|---|---|---|
| **7.56** | **1 567 ms freeze** | right at the lunar touchdown |
| 12.64 / 13.49 | 317 / 400 ms | leaving the Moon / Mars entry |
| 7.62, 11.61, 12.52, 12.65, 14.59 | 67–133 ms | site mounts / swaps |
These are mount-time costs (geometry generation, shader compiles, texture uploads, shadow-map changes), not steady-state fps.

## 1. Zero hitches (top priority)
- **Build everything before the visitor needs it:** generate Moon and Mars terrain geometry, rock instances and crater fields, and load all exhibit GLBs/textures **during the launch beats (0–6)** or in `requestIdleCallback` chunks, keep them mounted but `visible=false`, and **compile them** (`gl.compileAsync(scene, camera)` with them temporarily visible off-screen) before p reaches 6.5 (Moon) and 12.5 (Mars).
- **Never change shader-defining state mid-scroll:** toggling shadows on/off, changing light counts, switching `material.defines`, `transparent`, `fog`, or tier-dependent material swaps triggers recompiles — fix them at load.
- Don't resize shadow maps or render targets during the journey; don't create geometries/materials inside `useFrame` or on beat change.
- On low tier, prefer fewer objects over late mounting.
- Target: **no frame > 50 ms** anywhere from p = 6 to 21 on M1 (I will re-measure with the same script).

## 2. Cinematic, continuous camera
- **One continuous path:** replace the piecewise segments with a single smooth spline (centripetal Catmull-Rom or cubic Hermite) for camera **position and look-at**, C1-continuous through every keyframe; ease velocity to ~0 at rest beats (smoothstep/smootherstep in p), never a sudden change of direction, speed, or FOV.
- **Space → Moon landing (6 → 8):** one unbroken move — orbit view with the 5 pins → slow banking descent toward the Sea of Tranquility (the globe's curvature flattening into the horizon as we drop) → final approach skimming 20–30 m above the ground → gentle flare and settle (touchdown dust) → glide toward Apollo 11. No cuts, no fade-to-black, no sudden scale jumps between the globe and the surface terrain (cross-fade the globe into the surface while the horizon is in frame).
- **Site → site:** a smooth "hop": rise gently from the rest shot, **bank into a curve**, fly over the terrain at a steady speed with slight altitude change (crest a ridge or crater rim to *reveal* the next machine), then ease down into the next composed rest shot. No veil/dim, no teleport; if two sites use different terrain chunks, the swap must happen while the camera looks at a ridge/horizon that is identical in both.
- **Moon → Mars and Mars entry (12 → 14):** continuous lift-off → recede → cruise → entry plasma → descent → touchdown, same smoothness rules.
- Subtle life only: tiny camera roll in banks (≤ 3°), very slight handheld drift at rest (≤ 0.15°, off under reduced motion). No shake except ignition.

## 3. Remove Earth from the lunar surface
Hide Earth on every Moon surface beat and flight (p ≈ 7.5 → 12.6). It may stay in the space beats (launch, `deep-space` orbit view).

## 4. Less "plastic" (only if time remains after 1–3)
- Hardware materials: roughness/metalness variation, subtle normal detail (crinkled foil on LM and Surveyor, brushed metal on the LRV), small ambient occlusion in crevices, slight edge darkening; no flat uniform greys.
- Very light filmic grade on the Moon (cooler shadows, neutral highlights) and a touch of lens vignette — keep it subtle.

## Acceptance
- Re-measured: no frame > 50 ms from p = 6 to 21 (M1 high tier).
- Scrolling slowly from orbit to Apollo 11, and from Apollo 11 to Surveyor 3, looks like one continuous film shot.
- No Earth visible on the Moon surface.
