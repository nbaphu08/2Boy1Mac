## Goals
- **E1 Sky:** physically based dusk sky (sun ~3–5° above the western horizon, launch time 6:35 p.m. EDT): warm horizon glow → deep blue zenith, a soft broken cloud layer with sunlit edges and dark bellies, slight haze near the horizon. No flat gradient.
- **E2 Ocean & coast (pad 39B is right on the Atlantic):** water shader with moving normals, sun glint/specular path, distance fade into haze; a beach/dune line; **KSC wetlands** — low marsh grass/scrub (instanced, wind-sway subtle) around the crawlerway and pad perimeter.
- **E3 Pad 39B:** concrete pad with flame trench opening; **sound-suppression water tower** as the real tall cylindrical tank on legs (not a chrome ball); the **lightning-protection towers with catenary wires** spanning between them; mobile launcher tower with lattice detail, umbilical arms, warm work lights.
- **E4 Distance:** VAB at a believable distance (~5 km) softened by aerial perspective, the crawlerway leading toward it, a few low distant buildings; nothing pasted right behind the tower.
- **E5 Light & atmosphere:** warm low key light + cool sky fill; floodlights as soft volumetric glows and pools of light on the pad (no hard white cones); gentle fog/aerial perspective; a few tiny blinking aviation lights.
- **E6 Ignition (beat 2):** replace the bokeh-ball "steam" with billowing volumetric-looking steam clouds (layered soft sprites/noise volumes) rolling out of the flame trench, lit orange from below by the engines, white-grey on top; keep the shake.

`enter` and `ignition` look like a dusk photograph of LC-39B rather than a 3D mock-up; transitions into ascent unchanged and smooth; no new frame > 50 ms during beats 1–6; 0 console errors.
