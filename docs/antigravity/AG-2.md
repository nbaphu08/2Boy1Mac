## Goal
Seven museum-grade Mars dioramas with the shared Mars look (butterscotch haze sky, rusty regolith, rocks, dust; cold museum spotlight kept as the common language):
1. **Viking 1** — rock-strewn Chryse plain; existing model; RTG wind screens, dish, sampler arm visible.
2. **Pathfinder + Sojourner** — opened tetrahedral lander with deflated airbags and ramp; Sojourner a few meters away next to a rock (AG-3b models; lightbox until then).
3. **Spirit** — **wheels sunk to the hubs** in bright soft soil beside a low plateau (Home Plate); dusty.
4. **Opportunity** — heavy dust coat, darker hazy sky (storm mood), slope at a crater rim.
5. **Phoenix** — polygon-patterned arctic ground, trenches with bright ice, **one solar array cracked/collapsed** (AG-3b model).
6. **InSight** — smooth flat plain, **dust-covered solar panels**, seismometer **dome shield** and the mole housing on the ground in front.
7. **Ingenuity** — sand ripples of "Valinor Hills", **damaged rotor blade**; optional separate mini-plinth with Perseverance's backshell + parachute.
Also: smooth camera path along the Mars sequence; subtle airborne dust particles (more than the Moon, still light).
