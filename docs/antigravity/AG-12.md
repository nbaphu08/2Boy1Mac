## 2. Photo-real lunar surface, matched to two real Apollo photographs
References (in repo): `docs/antigravity/ref/moon-ref-AS15-86-11603.png` (Apollo 15, Jim Irwin and the rover, Mount Hadley behind) and `docs/antigravity/ref/moon-ref-AS16-113-18339.png` (Apollo 16, John Young's jump salute, Descartes highlands). Today our ground is a smooth, uniform grey plain; in the photos it is busy and textured. Match these features:
- **Regolith micro-relief everywhere:** fine powdery grain with a cauliflower/clumpy texture at close range; strong normal/height detail that reads at 1–20 m (use a displacement/normal combo; parallax or tessellated near-field patch on high tier; normal-only on low).
- **Many small craters at all scales:** soft-rimmed, eroded bowls from ~0.2 m to ~10 m, densely overlapping (instanced decals or procedural crater field in the height function), shadows filling their sunward side.
- **Scattered rocks and pebbles:** lots of 2–10 cm pebbles plus a few 20–50 cm angular rocks, half-buried, slightly lighter than the soil; distribution random, denser near crater rims.
- **Colour & tone:** neutral to warm grey with slight brown, **not** blue; the near ground is darker and grainier; lit slopes are bright, almost cream-white (see Mount Hadley / Stone Mountain in the photos); **opposition surge** — the ground looks brighter looking away from the sun.
- **Hard single sun, no fill:** deep black, sharp-edged shadows (rover/lander shadows exactly like the photos); shadowed sides of hills nearly black; no atmospheric haze at all — distant mountains are as crisp as near ones.
- **Distant mountains:** large, smooth, rounded massifs with soft gradients (like Mount Hadley / the Descartes ridges), not jagged peaks.
- **Sky:** pure black. Real lunar surface photos show **no stars** because of exposure — keep only a very faint star field (or none) on surface beats; keep the bright Earth where it is.
- **Disturbed soil around the hardware:** footprints and rover wheel tracks near the Apollo sites (Apollo 11, 15, 17) — darker, churned regolith; untouched soil at Surveyor 3 except around Apollo 12's visit path; **no footprints at GRAIL**.
- Keep Moon performance within tier budgets (≥ 55 fps M1 high, ≥ 30 fps phone low); texture/procedural resolution scaled per tier.

## Acceptance
