### Details
- **Ground-level rest shots:** camera ~1.6–3 m above the ground (eye height or a little higher), 35–50 mm feel, exhibit on a rule-of-thirds point, horizon in frame; keep **upper-left (narration) and lower-left (card)** areas calm/darker. Moon: black sky, hard low sun, long shadows. Mars: butterscotch dusty sky, aerial haze. Earth in the Moon sky only at near-side sites.
- **Use your AG-9b landscapes / AG-10 pilot** (ground to horizon, no plinths, no spotlight cones). One shared terrain system per planet is fine; each site gets its local cues from AG-9 §3–4.
- **Scale silhouette:** one reusable mesh, 1.8 m tall, placed beside each exhibit (not overlapping it); it must **not** look like an astronaut from a specific mission and must not carry flags/text. Sonnet shows the caption "a person 1.8 m tall, for scale".
- **Proximity export for Sonnet's tracker HUD:** expose a tiny read-only value (e.g. `components/journey/three/expeditionState.ts` → `expeditionState = { phase: 'orbit' | 'descent' | 'touchdown' | 'tracking' | 'arrived' | 'space', targetSlug: string | null, proximity: number /* 0 far … 1 arrived */ }`) updated every frame from `scrollState.current`. State the exact API in the handoff. No invented distances or numbers.
- **GRAIL (beat 12)** keeps your AG-10 simulation at rest; the low flight approaches the polar mountain.
- **Smoothness first:** scrolling fast or jumping via the rail/hash must never show a broken frame (cut to the destination pose if `|Δp| > 1.5` in one frame). Precompile terrain/materials before descent.
- **Reduced motion:** no dust, no streaking; the camera still follows scroll but may cut between rest shots.
