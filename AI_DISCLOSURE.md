# AI disclosure

SpaceMuse (Team 2Boy1Mac, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026) was built with AI assistance.

- **Anthropic Claude (Opus, Sonnet):** planning, research cross-checking, copywriting, application code, review.
- **Google Gemini (via Antigravity):** procedural 3D scenes and models drawn in code (Three.js).
- **ElevenLabs:** synthetic narration voice. The audio reads a script that the team wrote and verified.

Humans verified every fact against NASA sources. NASA photographs and imagery are used unmodified and credited. The 3D scenes are illustrative reconstructions generated by code, labelled "Illustrative 3D reconstruction · AI-assisted" on screen. They contain no NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers drawn by us.
